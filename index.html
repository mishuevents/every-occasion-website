Who we are

Decor that holds meaning, not just aesthetics.

Mishu founded Every Occasion Co. to bring the warmth of home into every celebration - pujas and housewarmings, engagements and haldis, milestone birthdays and full wedding weekends. Every backdrop, floral wall, and mandap is designed around the story of the people it's for, then built and styled on site down to the last garland.
